Call Summary & Scope

FULL TEXT HARVEST

The convergence of edge computing and autonomous cyber-physical systems mandates a foundational rethinking of machine learning deployment. Real-time control loops in industrial automation, robotic swarms, and decentralized power distribution can no longer tolerate high-latency roundtrips to centralized hyperscalers, nor can they withstand unmitigated network partitions.

This special issue aims to solicit original research covering principles, architectures, and experimental validations of resilient real-time edge intelligence. Particular focus is directed at resource-constrained platforms requiring bounded inference delays, multi-sensor calibration under adversarial perturbations, and distributed fault-tolerant model consensus.

Topics of Explicit Interest
  • Low-power deterministic inference engines
  • Sensor degradation resilience and dynamic re-calibration
  • Distributed consensus in intermittently connected edge fleets
  • Formal verification for edge neural controllers
  • Memory-bounded continual learning under domain drift
  • Hardware-software co-design for zero-trust industrial telemetry
